VyaparEase Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10/16/2026

This policy explains we handles information when you use the VyaparEase Android application. For privacy questions, contact innovations.lmtd@gmail.com.

At a glance

VyaparEase is an offline-first retail inventory and billing tool. It does not require an account and we do not operate an app server that receives your shop database. Information you enter is stored on your device unless you choose to export or share it, for example by saving a backup through Android’s document picker to a cloud-storage provider you select.

Information stored on your device

The app can store information you choose to enter, including:

  • Shop profile details such as shop name, address, phone number, state, GSTIN, GTIN, and food licence details.
  • Product catalogue and stock details such as product names, descriptions, SKU or barcode, category, unit, prices, quantities, and stock history.
  • Bill and return records such as invoice identifiers, items, quantities, prices, discounts, tax values and rates you enter, payment method/status, and optional customer name, address, GSTIN, and state.
  • Shop payment details you enter, such as a UPI ID/VPA, which can be encoded into a payment QR code.
  • Shop logo and signature images you select, app language, receipt settings, and other shop preferences.

This information is kept in the app’s private local storage. The app does not verify GSTIN, HSN, or user-entered tax rates, and does not send tax filings or payment instructions to a government service. It records payment status entered by the shop operator; it does not collect, transfer, or verify customer payments.

Barcode scanning

When you choose to scan a product barcode, VyaparEase uses Google Code Scanner through Google Play services. Google’s documentation says barcode image processing happens on the device and that Google does not store the scan image or result. The app receives the barcode result so it can find a matching product. The app enables the scanner’s auto-zoom feature; Google’s ML Kit disclosure describes limited scanner diagnostics for auto-zoom, including a dynamically generated scan-session identifier, zoom-level changes, and predicted barcode bounding-box coordinates. Google Play services may process this diagnostic information under Google’s terms and privacy disclosures. See Google ML Kit data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Backups, imports, and sharing

You can choose files and images using Android’s system document picker. If you create a backup, it contains shop settings and the catalogue, stock, billing, and payment-account data described above, including logo or signature data where present. The app writes the backup to the destination you choose. That destination may be a local folder or a third-party provider such as Google Drive. The provider handles the file after you save it, under its own terms and privacy policy. Backup files are not encrypted by VyaparEase; protect the destination and file accordingly.

Product imports, report exports, receipt printing, and payment QR display are initiated by you. When you print or share information through another app or service, that recipient handles the information under its own policies. We do not receive those files through a VyaparEase server.

How we use information

Information stored in the app is used on your device to manage products and stock, calculate bills from entered prices and tax settings, display reports, prepare receipts, and create or restore backups. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or selling data. VyaparEase does not include an app account system, advertising SDK, analytics service, or developer-operated cloud database.

Disclosure and sharing

We do not receive or sell the shop records stored by the app. Information may be processed by Android, Google Play services for barcode scanning and its described diagnostics, and any file, print, or sharing provider you select. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy notices. The app does not send billing records to customers’ payment apps; a displayed UPI QR is a payment handoff that the customer may scan using a separate service.

Retention and deletion

Local app records remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app’s storage/uninstall VyaparEase. To clear all local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → VyaparEase → Storage → Clear storage (wording can vary by device). Uninstalling also removes the app’s private local data. Exported backup files are separate copies: delete them from each destination or cloud-storage provider where you saved or shared them. Restoring a backup replaces the app’s current records on that device.

VyaparEase does not create user accounts, so there is no app account to request deletion for. If you contact us, we will use your message only to respond and will delete it when it is no longer needed, unless a legal obligation requires retention.

Security

App records are stored in Android’s private app storage. The app does not provide end-to-end encryption for records or exported JSON backups. You are responsible for securing the device, protecting exported backups, and choosing a trusted destination. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

VyaparEase is a business inventory and billing tool and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect children’s information through a developer-operated service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted at the same public URL with a revised effective date. Continued use after an update means the policy can be reviewed at that URL; it does not override rights or choices provided by applicable law.
